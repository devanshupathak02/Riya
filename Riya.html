Flagship Research Project

MBA Dissertation - Applying investment banking frameworks to real-world fintech challenges

Compliance Risk and Fintech Valuation: A Critical Case Study of Revolut Through Investment Banking Due Diligence Frameworks

Applies investment banking due diligence (Rosenbaum & Pearl, 2022) and valuation frameworks (DCF, comparable company analysis, venture capital method) to a real, live regulatory/valuation puzzle.
